# raw/park_brochures

Photographs of two maps of the park from its own brochures, from before 1996, taken by Jan de Kort (2026-11-21).
Every bungalow is drawn with its number and a symbol for its type.

- `IMG_4902.jpg` — the earlier map: 4 and 6 persons bungalows of type A and B, numbers 1 to 81.
- `IMG_4905.jpg` — the later map: the same, without 29, and type E, numbers 101 to 151.

The earlier map is the one of the old park; the later one already shows bungalows that came after it.

They are raw data: never edited. `data/old_park_brochures.json` holds what was read off them by hand;
`tools/make_old_park.py` fits them onto the topographic maps of `raw/topotijdreis`.
